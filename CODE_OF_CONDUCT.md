# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a respectful and welcoming environment for everyone contributing to REDGRID.

## Expected Behavior

Participants and contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Communicate constructively.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give appropriate credit to other contributors and third-party resources.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Threats or intimidation
- Plagiarism or misrepresentation
- Deliberate disruption of the project
- Sharing private or confidential information

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers privately.

Reports will be reviewed and handled appropriately.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project contributions, discussions, issues, pull requests, and other project-related interactions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action in response to violations, including removing inappropriate content or restricting participation.
